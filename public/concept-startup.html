① Double-dip: cap + discount

This SAFE has both a $8M cap and a 20% discount. Investors convert on whichever is better — you get diluted on the worse one. Standard is one or the other.

REMOVE DISCOUNT · EXPLAIN
② MFN with no sunset

The investor keeps "most favored" terms forever. Add a sunset (e.g. expires at your priced round) so it doesn't haunt later financings.

ADD SUNSET CLAUSE · EXPLAIN
③ Pro-rata rights — standard

Common at this stage. Fine to keep — just know it lets this investor maintain ownership in your next round.

WHAT WE'RE TRYING TO FIGURE OUT

This is an early concept — nothing is built yet. The idea: an AI tool that drafts and, more importantly, reviews the legal paperwork startups deal with, in plain English, for a fraction of a lawyer's cost.

If you'll humor us, the three questions we'd love a gut reaction to:

  • What's the legal-paperwork thing you most recently paid (or wish you hadn't paid) a lawyer for — that felt like it should've been a cheap tool?
  • Looking at screen 2 — would a "this clause is bad for you, here's why" review actually change what you'd do? Or do you already trust your lawyer / template for that?
  • What would make you not trust an AI for this?

Honest answers > nice ones — a flat "I'd never use this" is the most useful thing you could tell us. This is document automation, not legal advice, and not a real product yet.
